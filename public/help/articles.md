## start | Getting started | all | home
The portal is where you book sessions, boats and ergs, log your results and keep up with the club.

1. Open **vrc-rower-s-portal.web.app** and sign in with **Google** or your **email address** (we email you a one-tap sign-in link – no password).
2. The first time, find yourself in the member list and tap **This is me**. An admin confirms it, usually within a day, and you get an email.
3. Got an **invitation email**? Tap **Accept invitation** and sign in – you're linked straight away, no waiting.

> **Tip:** add the portal to your phone's home screen (Safari: Share → *Add to Home Screen*; Chrome: ⋮ → *Add to Home screen*). It opens like an app with the VRC icon.

## navigate | Finding your way around | all | home
- **On a computer** the menu is across the top: **Home**, **Programs ▾** (Weekly schedule, Open erg, Coxswains), **Training ▾** (Results, Leaderboard, Club challenges), **Fleet ▾** (Boats, Issues, Maintenance, Regattas), **Members** and **Feedback**. Admins also see **Admin**. The round **?** next to *My profile* opens these guides – it shows help for the page you were on.
- **On a phone** use the bar along the bottom (Home, Programs, Training, Fleet) and **Menu** for everything else – including **Help & how-to**.
- The **VRC logo** always takes you back Home.
- The version number at the bottom of every page (e.g. *v1.90*) tells you which version you're on. If something looks out of date, pull down / refresh the page.

## home | Your Home page | all | home
Home is your personal dashboard:
- **Quick actions** to book, log a result or open your profile.
- **Tiles** with your rowing kilometres this week (vs. the same point last week), sessions, this month vs. last month, and time on the water/erg.
- **Charts** of this week by day and your weekly / monthly trend – hover or tap a bar for the number.
- **Coming up**: your bookings (including when you're coxing), open erg slots, waitlists and maintenance crews you're on. A heads-up appears if one of your classes was cancelled.

## book | Booking a session | all | programs
1. Go to **Programs ▾ Weekly schedule** and pick a session – use ‹ › to change weeks.
2. On-water sessions: tap **Book a boat**, choose the boat class and boat (e.g. *4x · Sculling*), put yourself and your crew in the seats (bow → stroke) and add a cox if the boat needs one. Leave a seat **open** to let others join.
3. Erg sessions: tap **Sign me up** – you get the next free erg in your program's block.
4. Add it to your calendar when asked (Google, Outlook or a calendar file).

- You can book a program's sessions while you're **registered** in that program for the date (see *Programs, seasons and registration*).
- Booking opens **6 days before** (some programs differ) and closes when the session starts.
- See a boat with an **open seat**? Tap **Join**. Changed your mind? **Leave** keeps the rest of the crew booked.
- Boats that are out of service, being maintained or **away at a regatta** don't appear in the list.

## membership | Programs, seasons and registration | all | me
Programs like **Novice, Intermediate and Masters** run for a season from **1 October to 30 September** – written as e.g. **2026/27 Masters**.
- You can book a program's sessions (and open erg) only while you're **registered** in it for that date. Your results, leaderboards and history always stay visible.
- Your **Level** (e.g. Masters) is your rowing level – separate from the programs you're registered in this season.
- **My profile → Membership** shows your registrations, their dates and, if you pay by the quarter, the **paid-through** date. Booking pauses after it until an admin records your next payment.
- If you leave a program early, any bookings after your last day are cancelled with no fee and you get an email.
- Not registered or want to renew? Talk to a club admin.

## ergs | Erg sessions, erg numbers and the waitlist | all | session
Masters erg nights have two waves and 18 ergs, shared out by program:
- **Masters: ergs 1–12**, **Intermediate: 13–17**, **Para: erg 18 only**.
- You get the lowest free erg in your own block. At **noon on the day**, free ergs in the other blocks open to everyone (erg 18 always stays Para-only).
- The session page lists every erg with who's on it and the booking policy.

**Block full? Join the waitlist.** Each program has its own line. If someone cancels, their erg goes **automatically** to the first person waiting in that erg's group, and you get an email. You'll see *"You're #2 on the Masters waitlist"* on the session and on Home. Leave the waitlist any time.

## openerg | Open erg (outside programs) | all | openerg
Want to erg when there's no class? **Programs ▾ Open erg** (or **+ Open erg** on the schedule):
1. Pick a day (up to 6 days ahead) and a time between 06:00 and 21:30.
2. Choose how long (30 min to 2 h) – you get the lowest free erg (erg 18 is kept for Para). You need to be registered in a program for that date.
3. Add it to your calendar, and **Log result** afterwards. It counts in your history as *Open erg*.

## cancel | Cancelling, late cancels and no-shows | all | session
- Cancel from the session page (**Cancel** on your booking) or **Leave** if you're in someone else's crew. Please cancel as early as you can so someone else can row.
- Some programs charge a **late-cancel fee** (cancelling within a set number of hours before the start) and a **no-show fee** (booked but not checked in by the coach shortly after the start). The session page shows the exact rule, and you get a warning before a late cancel.
- A late-cancel fee is **waived automatically** if someone else takes the spot, or if the class itself is cancelled.
- You get an email whenever a fee is applied or removed. Think it's wrong? Talk to your coach or a club admin.

## cox | Coxswains: offering to cox | all | coxswains
- Tick **I can cox** on **My profile**, or ask an admin to give you the Cox role.
- Under **Programs ▾ Coxswains**, mark which on-water sessions you can cox over the next two weeks – one date at a time, or **Every week**.
- Crews booking a boat that needs a cox see available coxswains first. The Coxswains page also lists boats still looking for a cox.

## results | Logging results & Concept2 | all | results
1. **Training ▾ Results → + Log a result** (or **Log result** on your booking once the session has started).
2. Enter **distance** (switch between **km** and **m**) and **time**. Split per 500 m and watts are worked out for you; add strokes per minute, heart rate, calories or notes if you like.
3. Pick erg or on-water, and the machine – **bike / ski erg** pieces are listed but don't count towards rowing totals or leaderboards.

- Tap ♥ to like a teammate's result.
- **Concept2 Logbook:** on **My profile** tap **Connect Concept2** and approve. Your erg workouts then arrive automatically – nothing to type.

## leaderboard | Leaderboards | all | leaderboard
**Training ▾ Leaderboard** ranks members by kilometres, time, sessions attended or best 2k. Choose one or more programs (or **All programs**), the period and erg / water. The top three get a podium.

## challenges | Club challenges | all | challenges
**Training ▾ Club challenges** has the games and goals our coaches set up, for example *Erg to Hawaii*.
- **Team goal**: everyone's distance adds up – watch the 🚣 move past the milestones.
- **Leaderboard**: most distance, time or pieces wins.
- **Personal goal**: everyone aims for the same target and gets a progress bar.

There's **nothing extra to log** – the results you log (and Concept2 workouts) count automatically. Some challenges need you to tap **Join the challenge** first. Each one shows this week vs. the whole challenge, program standings and the latest pieces.

## fleet | Boats, rigging and boats away | all | fleet
- **Fleet ▾ Boats** shows every boat with its status, seats and rigs. **Blue** rig chips are sculling (1x, 2x, 4x…), **amber** are sweep (2-, 4+, 8+…); the tag shows how it's rigged right now. Filter by status or Sculling / Sweep.
- **📅 Boats away** shows which boats are going to regattas over the next 12 weeks. Boats away can't be booked – if you'd already booked one you'll get an email asking you to pick another.

## damage | Reporting damage | all | issues
Something broken? **Fleet ▾ Issues → Report damage**:
1. Pick the boat, describe the problem and add photos.
2. Mark it **unsafe** if nobody should row it – admins get an urgent email straight away.

## maintenance | Maintenance crews | all | maintenance
Admins schedule maintenance jobs (waxing, repairs, re-rigging) under **Fleet ▾ Maintenance**. Tap **I can help** to join a crew, add it to your calendar, and **Mark done** when finished. A boat being worked on is hidden from booking for that time.

## regattas-member | Regattas: going, shifts and the info pack | all | regattas
**Fleet ▾ Regattas** lists the regattas the committee has published.
- **Are you going?** Tap Going / Maybe / Not this time, add events you'd like to race, and tick if you're coming to help or spectate. *⭐ Recommended for you* means your coach thinks you should enter.
- **Your races** shows your events, race times, boat and crew.
- **Loading shifts:** everyone going is asked to do a shift (loading / unloading the trailer at VRC or the venue). Tap **Sign me up**, **+ my crew** to sign your crew up together, or **Cover for…** to take someone's shift. Each shift emails you a **calendar invite that updates itself** if the time changes, plus a reminder the day before. Can't make it? **Can't make it** takes you off – please find a substitute.
- **Trailer drivers** need to be approved – tap **Ask to be an approved driver**.
- **Your own equipment** (your single, your blades…) going on the club trailer? Add it so it's on the load list and checked out and back.
- **Info pack:** schedule, meeting point, venue map, parking, cox meeting, videos and the photo album.

## profile | My profile & privacy | all | me
**My profile** (top right) is where you:
- add a photo, your sides (stroke / bow / both), experience and a short bio;
- set your **racing category** and **birth year** (for regatta Masters age bands – other members only see the band, e.g. *Masters D*);
- update your **contact details, emergency contact and medical notes** – only you and the admins see these;
- see your **member number**, attendance, results and session history;
- connect **Concept2** and choose your calendar.

## feedback | Feedback, bugs and ideas | all | feedback
Help us make the portal better under **Feedback**:
- **🐞 Report a bug** – what went wrong, the steps, and what you expected. A screenshot helps a lot.
- **💡 Request a feature** or **💬 Feedback**.
- Everyone can see the reports and give a **▲ +1** to the ones they care about. Each report shows its status (New, Planned, In progress, Done, Won't do) and any reply from the admins.

## emails | Emails you might get | all |
- **Welcome** when your profile is approved, or an **invitation** from an admin.
- **Class cancelled** (with the reason) or reinstated.
- **Waitlist**: an erg opened up and it's yours.
- **Boat away**: a boat you booked is going to a regatta – please pick another.
- **Fees**: a late-cancel / no-show fee was applied or removed.
- **Regatta shifts**: calendar invites, time changes and day-before reminders.

## trouble | Something not working? | all |
- **Refresh** the page (or close and reopen the home-screen app) – you'll pick up the latest version.
- Check the **version** at the bottom of the page.
- Signed in but only see "Find your member profile"? Your request is waiting for an admin.
- Still stuck? **Feedback → Report a bug** – the details of your phone or computer are added automatically.

## coach-checkin | Checking in athletes (attendance) | coach | session
From the day of the session, open it on the schedule and tap **✓ Check in athletes**:
- Mark each person **Present**, **Late** or **No-show**, add **walk-ins**, or **Mark the rest present**.
- Attendance shows on member profiles and counts towards the Sessions leaderboard.
- If your program charges **no-show fees**, anyone booked who isn't checked in when class closes (10 min after the start by default) is charged – so please check in at every session. No check-in at all means nobody is charged.

## coach-cancel | Cancelling a class | coach | session
Coaches and admins see **Cancel class** on a session page (today or later):
1. Pick the reason (Air quality, Wind, Lightning, Holiday, Other) and add notes.
2. Leave **Email the people signed up** ticked – everyone booked (crews and coxes) gets an email.
**Reinstate class** undoes it and can email everyone too. Late-cancel fees for that class are waived.

## coach-challenges | Building a club challenge | coach | challenges
**Training ▾ Club challenges → + New challenge.** Start from a template (Erg to Hawaii, Kilometre King & Queen, 100 km club, Show-up challenge) or blank, then set:
- the type (team goal, leaderboard, personal goal), measure (km, minutes, pieces) and target;
- start and end dates, erg / water / both, and whether bike and ski erg count;
- which programs take part, and whether members must tap **Join**;
- for team goals, the **milestones** – one per line, `Place | distance`.
Results already logged in the dates count straight away.

## committee-plan | Planning a regatta (committee) | committee | regattas
The Regatta committee and Dockmaster (and admins) see the planning side of **Fleet ▾ Regattas**. *Coming up* lists every future regatta; the year calendar shows them all – click a date to add one.

Each regatta has tabs:
- **Overview** – boats away dates, files (entries, line-ups, bookings) and any member bookings that clash with boats going away.
- **Entries** – add entries one by one or **📋 Paste entries** from RegattaCentral / a spreadsheet. **Checks** flag boat and rower clashes, empty seats, re-rigs between races, and crews that don't match the event (W / M / Mixed, Masters band).
- **People** – who's going, ⭐ recommendations, shifts done vs. needed, and trailer driver approvals.
- **Shifts** – add shifts (or **Add the usual 4 shifts**), see and change who's on each. Changing a time updates everyone's calendar.
- **Info pack** – tick **Published to members** and fill in the schedule, meeting point, venue, cox meeting, videos and album.
- **Trailer & checklist** – put boats on trailers, choose drivers, tick off the checklist, print the **load list**.
- **Dockmaster** – tick each boat, oar set and private item **Out** and **Back**.
- **Reports** – blades needed at once (+ spares), riggers, and printable **Equipment allocation** and **Loading checklist**.
- **Costs** – entry fees, truck, trailer and fuel (estimate vs. actual) and each person's share, with a CSV for billing.

> **⚙ Timing & spares** sets how long crews are on the water around their race, spare blades and riggers, and the entry deadline (you get reminders 7 and 2 days before).

## admin-page | The Admin page | admin | admin
- **Requests** to approve or decline (the red badge in the menu). Check the member number ✓ / ✗ against the club list.
- **Club admins** roster, **Portal version** and recent emails.
- Email switches for damage reports, join requests and the weekly fee report.
- **No-show & late-cancel fees** per program, the week's fees (Waive / Re-apply) and **Email me this report now**.
- **Developer feedback tab** switch to show or hide Feedback from members.

## admin-members | Adding & inviting members, roles | admin | members
- **Members → + Add member**: fill in the name, programs and email, and leave **Email an invitation** ticked – they get a one-time link that signs them straight in (valid 14 days).
- On anyone not signed in yet: **✉ Invite to the portal / Resend invitation**.
- **Edit member → Level** (Novice / Intermediate / Masters…) – programs come from their registrations.
- **Edit member → Roles**: Rower, **Coach** (check-in, cancel classes, challenges), Cox, **Regatta committee**, **Dockmaster**.
- Admins can search the directory by **member number**, merge duplicates and unlink accounts.

## admin-registrations | Registering members each season | admin | admin
Members can only book programs they're registered in. **Admin page → Program registrations**:
- **Register several members**: pick the program (and a *paid-through* date for quarterly payers), tick the names, **Register**.
- **Payments due or lapsed** lists quarterly payers whose paid-through date is within 14 days or past – **+1 quarter** extends to the next quarter end (31 Dec, 31 Mar, 30 Jun, 30 Sep).
- **Registered last season but not this one** helps with renewals at the start of October.
- On a member's profile, **Membership → + Register**, **Edit**, **+1 quarter** or **End early** (last day + reason – their later bookings are cancelled with no fee and they're emailed).
- **One program per member at a time.** Registering someone in a new program ends the old one the day before (no email). If anyone ends up in two, the Admin page offers **Keep the highest** (Junior → Novice → Intermediate → Masters).
- New members: **+ Add member** has *Register for 2026/27*; approving a join request registers them in the highest program they asked for.
- A member's **Level** defaults to the highest program on file and can be changed under Edit member; it's separate from their registrations. Who can book is recalculated every night (or **↻ Recalculate who can book now**).

## admin-programs | Programs, sessions and erg blocks | admin | manage
**Programs ▾ Weekly schedule → Manage programs** to add or edit programs (colour, how many days ahead sign-ups open, boat required, PCOC) and their weekly sessions (day, time, type, capacity, who's eligible, coach). Use **Edit this date** to change one date's coach or capacity. Erg sessions have **erg blocks** (e.g. `Masters 1-12; Intermediate 13-17; Para 18 only`) and the time they open to everyone.

## admin-feedback | Handling feedback | admin | feedback
On **Feedback**, use **Status / reply** to set a report's status and add a note everyone sees. Tick reports (or **Select all shown**) and **📋 Copy for Claude** – paste it into a chat with Claude to have the changes made, with a preview link to check before they go live.
